# ai-experiments-and-research
Independent AI research focused on practical and lightweight experiments, human AI interaction, simple model behaviors, and exploratory studies in machine learning. A collection of small-scale analyses, prototypes, and observations aimed at understanding how AI systems interact with users and real world scenarios.

🧠 Overview
---
This repository serves as a personal research space where I explore:
* Human & AI interaction
* Simple and interpretable model behaviors
* Lightweight, practical AI experiments
* Observational studies and idea-driven prototypes
* Small-scale tests to understand real-world AI behavior

The goal is not to build large production systems, but to experiment, observe, and document meaningful patterns in AI behavior.

🔍 Research Focus Areas
---
Human & AI Interaction
Understanding communication patterns and behavior shaping between users and AI.

Simple and Interpretable AI Behaviors
---
Minimal examples, edge cases, and behavior-driven tests.

Practical / Lightweight Experiments
---
Quick, real world oriented tests and small prototype interactions.

📝 Goals
---
* Understand AI behavior in simple, everyday scenarios
* Document insights, failures, and patterns
* Explore communication and alignment
* Build small, interpretable, reusable experiments
